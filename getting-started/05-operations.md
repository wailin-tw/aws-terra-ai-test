# Operations

Operations covers the day-2 work needed to keep the platform healthy after it is deployed.

---

## Operational focus areas

- Monitoring and alerting.
- Incident response and escalation.
- Backup and restore testing.
- Patch and maintenance planning.
- Cost and usage review.
- Access review and audit readiness.

---

## Required operational outcomes

| Area | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Observability | Issues are visible before users report them |
| Reliability | Services have retry, failover, and rollback paths where needed |
| Recovery | Backups are restorable and restore drills are practiced |
| Security | Access and logs support audits and incident investigations |
| Cost | Unused resources are identified and removed regularly |

---

## Incident response basics

1. Detect the issue.
2. Triage impact and scope.
3. Mitigate or rollback.
4. Communicate status to stakeholders.
5. Capture root cause and follow-up actions.
